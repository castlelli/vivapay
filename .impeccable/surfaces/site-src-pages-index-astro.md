Scope: VivaPay showcase landing page (EN `/vivapay/`, PT `/vivapay/pt/`). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: founders, investors and industry people, event organizers, and recruiters coming from the portfolio. Action: "Running an event? Let's talk" (email/LinkedIn). Proof: R$10K+, 5,000+ people, 10,000 transactions, 40 booths, 3 days, team of 7. Constraints: no pricing, no JS required, reduced motion honored, self-hosted fonts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The voucher card is the product's credential and the page's hero object. The page refuses the fintech default of a dark hero with a floating phone and a logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: Deep forest (#11201B) and paper (#EDEAE2) grounds, with champagne (#D5C6A1) for emphasis and sage (#93A39B) for secondary text. Schibsted Grotesk sets the display (700 against 400 in champagne). Fragment Mono is used only for money, counts and serials. Flat paper surfaces, hairline rules, a perforation line. No gradients except photo scrims, no glass. Photos are tinted into the forest palette.

STORY: A visitor sees a premium card at a fair, the claim "One card. Zero cash." and the real totals. They understand the loop (top up, then scan to pay, then close the books), trust the ledger mechanism, and contact the author.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed night-fair photo with the green card on the counter, right of center, carrying the wordmark. The two-line headline sits left at about 128px with the lede and CTA below it. A four-column stat rail is pinned to the bottom edge. Nav: wordmark left, links and EN/PT right.

FORM: Grounded candidate 1 (Credential; the user chose it over the rolled Final Board). seed:31a8745cca6e9f3d. Signature interaction: the ledger replay. Voucher rows append one by one and the derived balance recomputes as the ledger scrolls into view.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
